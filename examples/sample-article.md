Electrolyte losses reached 32% of recovered material in 2021. Most recyclers lack a low-energy separation step (Lee, 2021). Kim and Park (2019) showed that membranes can separate carbonate solvents, and we delve into a robust process that uses profound learning to tune the membrane.
